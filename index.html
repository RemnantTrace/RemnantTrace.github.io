01 / ABOUT

Researching what technology leaves behind.

RemnantTrace Forensics is an independent digital forensics research laboratory focused on digital evidence, forensic methodologies, and emerging technologies.

Our work examines the artifacts and traces produced by computers, mobile devices, cloud platforms, applications, and connected technologies through controlled research and technical analysis.
