FOUNDER & CEO

Meg Sissel is the Founder and CEO of RemnantTrace Forensics, an independent research laboratory focused on digital evidence, digital forensics, forensic methodology, and emerging technologies.

Her professional background spans software engineering, bioinformatics, security operations, technical training, incident-response workflow development, litigation support, and digital forensics. This multidisciplinary background informs RemnantTrace's approach to forensic research: examining not only what evidence exists, but how it is created, stored, modified, synchronized, recovered, and ultimately interpreted.

02 / BACKGROUND

Megan studied Software Engineering and Bioinformatics, building a foundation in programming, computational systems, databases, and data analysis. Her professional background began in security support and operations before moving into technical training and operational systems. From 2023 to 2024, she worked as a Technical Trainer supporting Volvo Trucks North America, Mack Trucks, Penta, and Prevost. Her work extended beyond technical instruction into workflow development, incident-response operations, and database development for managing case workflows within live call-center environments, working alongside Security Operations Center personnel and engineering teams.

In 2025, Megan moved into digital forensics and litigation support with Reliance Forensics, working with digital evidence in support of criminal and civil matters. Her experience included the examination, preservation, and analysis of digital evidence across matters involving criminal investigations, employment disputes, workplace investigations, fraud, unauthorized access, and other litigation. She currently works as a Forensics Analyst at CyberNarus in Minnesota, continuing her professional work in digital forensics and digital evidence analysis.

In 2026, Megan founded RemnantTrace Forensics as an independent research platform focused on digital evidence, forensic methodology, and emerging technologies. RemnantTrace was created to provide a space for conducting, documenting, and publishing independent research, with an emphasis on controlled experimentation, repeatable methods, and understanding the technical conditions behind digital artifacts.

04 / THE MISSION

Independent research into the traces technology leaves behind.

RemnantTrace exists to investigate questions that sit at the intersection of technology and forensic science.

The laboratory's work is centered on experimentation, reproducibility, documentation, and transparency. Research examines digital systems under defined conditions to better understand the evidence they produce and the limitations associated with interpreting that evidence.
