02 / RESEARCH

Areas of investigation.

Research projects explore the technical conditions that produce, preserve, alter, and expose digital traces.

RESEARCH AREAS
01 Digital Forensics READ MORE

Examining forensic artifacts, acquisition methods, analysis techniques, and the behavior of digital evidence.

Research may examine where artifacts are created, how they change, what survives common user actions, and what acquisition or analysis conditions affect their visibility.

02 Digital Evidence READ MORE

Studying how information is created, stored, synchronized, recovered, interpreted, and retained across digital systems.

Research considers the transitions between creation, storage, synchronization, modification, deletion, recovery, and interpretation.

03 Emerging Technology READ MORE

Investigating new platforms, applications, devices, services, and technologies as their forensic footprints develop.

Research can document unfamiliar artifacts and behaviors before they become well understood by the broader forensic community.

04 Forensic Methodology READ MORE

Testing repeatable approaches and documenting the conditions, limitations, and observations behind research findings.

Methods should identify inputs, environment, actions, acquisition conditions, observations, and limitations so results can be evaluated and repeated.

PROJECT MODEL

Observe. Test. Document. Repeat.

Projects are structured around defined questions, controlled conditions, documented observations, and transparent limitations.
