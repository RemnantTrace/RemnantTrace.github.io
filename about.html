Inside the laboratory.

RemnantTrace Forensics is an independent digital forensics research laboratory focused on understanding the traces technology leaves behind.

MISSION

Understand the evidence before drawing conclusions from it.

Research focuses on observing how digital artifacts are produced, modified, synchronized, preserved, recovered, and interpreted.

Work is designed around controlled experimentation, documentation, repeatability, and clear communication of limitations.

SCOPE

Computers

Operating systems, applications, filesystems, metadata, and user activity.

Mobile

Mobile operating systems, applications, backups, synchronization, and device artifacts.

Cloud

Cloud platforms, account activity, synchronization behavior, and retained artifacts.

Emerging Tech

New technologies whose forensic footprints are still developing.

CURRENT MODEL

Independent research first.

RemnantTrace currently operates as a research-focused laboratory rather than accepting client forensic examinations or litigation matters. The goal is to develop useful research, datasets, methods, and technical knowledge.
